## Pass 1: hostile editor on v1, against the readers' notes

3. Rent control sat in quest two as "one thing does get cheap". It is not something that holds value. Moved to the landlords in quest four, where it is the reason their win is not clean.

### Unsupported or overstated in v1
4. "Germany's own statistics office printed them in nineteen twenty-five", said of "the prices": only the monthly Berlin bread series is the statistics office's. The loaf prices in October are a letter-writer's.
5. "because Germany is behind on deliveries": the museum's words are that the troops came to force bigger deliveries of timber and coal. The default is in a 1924 US government compilation; the narration keeps to the museum.
6. "A woman who was nine that year": born 1914, month unknown. "A child."
7. "By August prices move by the hour": the historian's "hourly" carries no month. Cut; the coffee shows it.
8. "A few slices" for 145 grams: an illustration, marked as such in the fact table.
9. "tells a council": the body is not identified in the only source read.
10. "A photo agency shows what people do": the evidence is the caption.
11. "crossed from France into the German town of Kehl and changed ten francs": no fragment says where he changed them.
12. "Tradesmen start asking for food and coal": the Bundesbank's sentence is about some traders, and it is today's bank looking back.
13. "she lists three days of bread": the letter does not say three days. "As it climbs."
14. "the state has just paid them off for fifteen pfennigs": the debt was not paid or cancelled; the 1925 law converted old bonds at 25 marks per 1,000. "Ends up worth."
15. "A historian of Bavaria lists the winners": it is a Bavarian historical encyclopedia.
16. "His group swells to": the museum's count has no date and says "comprises".
17. "one verdict from nineteen thirty stands": the chapter page does not print the author or year.
18. "What matters is the hard limit": Sargent's reading, given as the narrator's. Reduced to the fact of a limit.
19. "Savers are furious" (in the outline, not the draft): no source. Not written.
20. Fifteen pfennigs given without "in pre-war money".
