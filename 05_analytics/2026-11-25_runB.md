## Built: EP006, What Did Vikings Actually Do All Winter?
- A farm in central Norway, about AD 950, daily-life pillar. Rule 10: a different era and part of the world from EP002 to EP005, and EP005 was a point-of-view episode.
- `engine/ep06.py`: 8:33, 105 narration lines, 227 stills, 89.6 MB.
- **The narration is the run A script word for word except nine lines**, changed because of what was opened or read today (the as-recorded text is `02_script/EP006_script_v3_as_recorded.txt`):
  - The Yule ale law (two lines). Run A left "translators differ on whether it's the ale, or the grain" to be settled here. Two translations of Heimskringla were opened and both make the measure grain or malt ("brew a meal of malt into ale"; "ale for the celebration with a measure of grain, or else pay fines"). The "measure of ale" comes from two retellings. The lines now say that, and put the fine where the translation puts it.
  - Svein "raids again until a month into winter" (was "until winter has begun"; the saga has him away "till the first month of winter was ended"). Serious: the old line told the viewer the opposite.
  - "And it was mostly women who made the sail" (was "The women made the sail"; the sources say typically and mostly). Serious.
  - Stockfish "as much as ten years later"; "the winter of twenty eleven to twelve"; "a warning against gambling with dice" (no longer voiced as a quotation); "the wool of a hundred and fifty sheep, to a year's wool from about seven hundred"; "a very big ship, its sail and a hundred men" (the 84 years covers the sail, and the source does not call the ship a warship).
- New props in `engine/thsq.py` (new methods only): longhouse, pine, mountain range, haycock, pig, split cod and a drying rack, bone skate, ski, gaming board, die, gaming piece, drinking horn, warp-weighted loom, spindle, longship (sail sets and furls; plain stems), vat, lamp, sock, round shield, bolt of cloth, lyre, snowfall, a sketch map of northern Europe. In `ep06.py`: the hall seen from inside with its long fire, smoke hole, wall bench and haze; the farm from outside by day, dusk and night; a frozen lake; a northern shore; a winter bar in the corner that fills over the 180 days.
- Opening (the lesson of the EP001 and EP003 curves): no text card. The first frame is a close view of Sid asleep on the bench under a blanket with smoke crossing the frame; his eyes open at one second. The four and a half hours and the 180 days are both spoken inside 14 seconds.
- Movement: fires flicker and their smoke climbs to the roof hole; haze drifts in every hall scene; snow and rain fall; breath shows outdoors; the sun crosses its low arc and sets; skaters cross the lake; the cod swing on the rack; spindles turn and cloth grows on the loom; ships sail, launch and furl; the cod's route and every counter draw themselves (180, 13 weeks, 42 skates, 10 years, 80 men, 150 and 700 sheep, 100 men, 84 years).
