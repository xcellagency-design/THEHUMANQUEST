- "Is hyperinflation a one-way street?"

**"Hyperinflation During Germany's Weimar Republic Years"**, Straight Dope, 26 Jan 2011, 19 replies on page one: https://boards.straightdope.com/t/hyperinflation-during-germanys-weimar-republic-years/569120 (page text)

- Was it deliberate, and who gained: one poster argued it was engineered to wipe out industrial debts and named Hugo Stinnes; others disputed it
- Did it wipe out reparations? Corrected in-thread: "German war reparations were not denominated in paper marks."
