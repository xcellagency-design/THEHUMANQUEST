## Built: EP005, You Joined Magellan's Voyage in 1519. Only 18 Finished It.
- At sea from Spain to the Spice Islands and home, 1519 to 1522, point-of-view pillar. Rule 10: a different era and part of the world from EP001 to EP004; EP004 was a money episode.
- `engine/ep05.py`: 8:11, 116 narration lines (the v2 script word for word, with four respellings for the voice), 256 stills, 89.7 MB.
