## EP005: You Signed Up for Magellan's Voyage. 18 Came Home (topic 5, point-of-view pillar, score 85)
Why this one:
- **Rule 10.** 1519 to 1522, the Atlantic, Patagonia, the Pacific, the Philippines and the Spice Islands. EP001 Egypt 2560 BC, EP002 New York 1929, EP003 Roman Italy AD 79, EP004 England 1349: a different era and part of the world from all four. Point-of-view pillar; EP004 was money.
- **Score.** Second in `topic-next` (85). First is the Odyssey from the crew's point of view (86). The Odyssey was passed over because it is a third ancient Mediterranean setting in five episodes, with the same ships and tunics as Pompeii, which is the sameness the owner objected to; and because it is a poem, so it cannot supply the checkable facts this channel's best Shorts are made of. Its case is the film (above) and the 1.4M Odyssey video on a channel the owner named. It stays at the top of the list.
- **Market.** The table above: crews, counts and survival at sea, on small channels, this month.
- **Our own numbers.** The Shorts that were shown to people each carried one concrete, checkable fact, and the only one watched to the end and round again opened on a physical problem. This voyage has at least five: a rat for half a ducat; the day that went missing; the mutineer who brought the ship home; 381 sacks of cloves that paid for five ships; the ship that turned round and got home first.
- **The opening.** EP001 lost half its viewers in 20 seconds on a myth-and-source opening (`05_analytics/2026-11-20.md`). This story lets the first sentence be a man eating part of his ship.
- **It can be checked.** The eyewitness account was readable in three translations, with two university pages, a national archive's exhibition and two magazine articles beside it.

Working title: **You Signed Up for Magellan's Voyage. 18 Came Home.** Run B decides the final title and thumbnail. Title shapes the research favours: a number of men, a year.

## What the research changed
- **"18 came home" is the famous number and it is not the whole truth.** Eighteen finished on the Victoria. The San Antonio turned back in the strait and reached Seville in May 1521; twelve men taken at Cape Verde and four or five from the Trinidad are also counted as survivors. About ninety men saw Spain again. The episode asks "are you one of the eighteen?" and then widens the count. The title stays accurate as long as the description says "came home on the Victoria".
- **The hidden fourth quest is that nobody planned to go round the world.** Three sources say so in terms. The Victoria went west from the Spice Islands because the flagship leaked.
- **The headcount at departure is disputed** (237 to 270). The script says so.
- **The mutiny cannot be told in detail.** Pigafetta's own translators disagree about who was beheaded. The script keeps to what a university page and two others agree on.
