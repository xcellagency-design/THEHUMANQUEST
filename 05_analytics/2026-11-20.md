## What the evidence supports
1. **The EP001 hook failed, by the manual's own test.** Nearly half the viewers were gone by 0:20 and almost two thirds by 0:34. In that first 20 seconds the script said: you were told slaves built the pyramids; whips and chains; that picture comes from a Greek writer; the builders left a diary. The viewer was not put into the scene until the seventh line ("So let's make you one of them"), about 30 seconds in.
2. **Part of that loss cannot be the script.** 28% left inside 7 seconds, when one and a half sentences had been spoken. That is a reaction to the first frames, the voice or a mismatch with the title, and this data cannot say which. The owner has called the animation poor more than once; this is consistent with that and does not prove it.
3. **The people who stayed past two minutes stayed to the end.** From 2:16 to the end the line is nearly flat (14% to 12%), and against similar-length videos the ending scores above typical (0.69). The body of the episode holds the viewers the opening does not lose.
4. **Shorts: the wet-sand Short is the only one watched past its own length (122%, so people let it loop).** It is also the most viewed. Its first words put the viewer in a physical problem ("And you have rope...") and its title names a trick. The worst-held Short, "The Lost City" (27%, 7 seconds of 26), opens on "You expect a camp. You get a city.": a claim, with nothing to look at yet. Two videos, so this is a lead to test, not a rule.
5. **Tuesday's "barely shown" Shorts were only delayed.** "One Pyramid Block Every 2 Minutes" went from 1 view to 112 and brought the channel's first subscriber and first comment. "Signed Their Work" went from 0 to 52, "The Lost City" from 3 to 43. Tuesday's note read the early zeros as possibly a refusal; that reading was wrong. A Short's first day on this channel says little.
6. **The first non-pyramid Short got 61 views in six hours** (mattress, 1929). On Tuesday evening four of the seven pyramid Shorts then public had 3 views or fewer. One video, six hours: noted, nothing more.
7. **Long videos are hardly being shown at all**: 52, 20 and 1 views. With impressions unavailable, low reach and low click-through cannot be told apart. EP002's single view in 26 hours is the weakest start so far; it went out at 7 PM, seven hours after EP003, on a channel with one subscriber.

Confidence: **moderate** on point 1 (clear shape, small sample, one video), **low** on everything else.
