# EP005 editorial pass on v1 (hostile-editor read), 20 Nov 2026

Line numbers are 0-based rows of `EP005_script_v1.txt`. Every point was checked against a source opened in this run (see `01_facts/facts.json`). v1 was written from memory of the story before the sources were read line by line; this pass is where memory lost.

## The opening (changed because of the EP001 retention curve)
1. Lines 0-2 run to about 53 words, roughly 16 seconds. The stakes must land inside 10. Cut to three short sentences (32 words in the final text, just under 10 seconds at EP004's pace of 198 words a minute): the hide, the rat, five ships and eighteen men.
2. Line 0: "off the ship's rigging" is not what the source says. Pigafetta: ox hides that covered the top of the main yard. In the cold open, "used to be part of the ship" is true and quicker; the main yard is named when the scene comes round again.
3. Line 2: "One ship gets home" is contradicted by line 107 (the San Antonio got home in 1521). Say one ship finishes the voyage.
4. Lines 10-20: eleven lines of set-up before the ships move, about 50 seconds. EP001 lost two viewers in three by 0:34. Cut the route explanation and the nationalities list; keep cloves, the price, Magellan, recruiting, the ship, the date.

## Unsupported or overstated

7. Line 6: "the wall" is my image. Say a continent.

9. Line 16: only two figures were found: 24 Portuguese and 27 Italians, with under two thirds of the crew Spanish. Greeks and Frenchmen as groups were not verified. Use the proportion.
10. Line 17: "paid four months in advance" was not found. The archive pages opened give no pay by rank. Cut. "Expected to last two years" is in the archive text; keep.
